# Luxury Clienteling Business Understanding

**Status:** Confirmed principles for Design v0.1.

## Relationship first, transaction second

Long-term trust, service quality, and emotional value matter alongside commercial outcomes. Immediate conversion is not the sole objective.

## Personalization should be felt, not displayed

Client information should guide better choices, not be recited back to prove that the system possesses a profile.

## Right message, right context, right moment

Quality includes whether contact is appropriate now, what should be said, and how it should be expressed.

## Exclusivity must be grounded

Real previews, events, and services can support a sense of exclusivity. The system must not invent VIP rights, inventory, scarcity, event capacity, discounts, or service commitments.

## Human in the loop

The Client Advisor remains accountable for the final message and sending decision.

## Diversity never overrides appropriateness

The three candidates should offer meaningful choice, but every approach must remain relevant, factually consistent, and suitable for the current relationship context.
