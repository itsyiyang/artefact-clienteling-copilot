# Architecture Decision Record Index — Design v0.1

Status vocabulary: **Accepted**, **Principle accepted / details pending**, **Proposed**, and **Open**.

| ID | Decision | Status | Rationale / boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| ADR-001 | Position the product as a Luxury Clienteling-aware AI Copilot | Accepted | Differentiation comes from relationship and context awareness, not fluent text alone. |
| ADR-002 | Prioritize long-term relationship value, not only immediate conversion | Accepted | Luxury clienteling depends on trust, service experience, and client autonomy. |
| ADR-003 | Use an explicit strategy pipeline rather than only direct generation | Accepted | Structured intermediate outputs support control, evaluation, and defect attribution. |
| ADR-004 | Use Scenario-aware Client Understanding | Accepted | Focus on information relevant to the current interaction while checking cross-scenario boundaries. |
| ADR-005 | Use one shared objective plus three Dynamic Approaches | Accepted | Candidates should offer meaningful choices without contradicting facts or the relationship goal. |
| ADR-006 | Separate Understanding, Planning, Generation, and Validation responsibilities | Accepted | Separation makes errors easier to locate and each capability independently evaluable. |
| ADR-007 | Separate observed facts, supported interpretations, and unknowns | Accepted | Prevents guesses from silently becoming client facts. |
| ADR-008 | Apply Brand Policy across all stages | Principle accepted / details pending | Brand and grounding rules should guide decisions before final validation; exact policy is open. |
| ADR-009 | Include Communication Eligibility in Planning | Proposed | `appropriate`, `caution`, and `not_recommended` are useful, but output behavior is unresolved. |
| ADR-010 | Choose LLM-call topology and agent/framework usage | Open | Logical stages do not imply separate calls, agents, or a specific framework. |
| ADR-011 | Define Brand Identity & Policy | Open | Must be distinctive, executable, and evaluable; this is the next design topic. |

These records preserve what has and has not been decided. A future decision should be added as a new ADR or an explicit superseding record rather than silently rewriting history.
