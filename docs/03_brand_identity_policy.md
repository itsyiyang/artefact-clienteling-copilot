# Brand Identity & Policy

**Status:** Principles accepted; operational details pending (2026-11-09).

最新共识见 [rules.md](../rules.md)，六场景讨论示例见 [scenario_rule_review.md](scenario_rule_review.md)。已确认皮具、精细工艺与长期使用、克制而有辨识度的设计、阶段性系列表达，以及主动服务但尊重客户自主性。客户等级只在影响明确权益或资格时使用。名称、产品事实、服务条款及Judge阈值仍未冻结。

Brand Policy constrains Understanding, Planning, Generation, and Validation. The Markdown rules are a discussion baseline, not a deployed executable policy.

## Topics to decide

- Brand values and distinctive client-relationship philosophy
- Voice, tone, vocabulary, and Chinese WeChat conventions
- Service posture and acceptable calls to action
- Grounding rules for products, events, availability, benefits, and pricing
- Privacy and personalization boundaries
- Scenario-specific allowed, discouraged, and prohibited behaviors
- Escalation and human-review conditions
- How each rule can be evaluated consistently

Until these are agreed, do not create a production `brand_policy.yaml` or claim that messages are “on-brand.”
